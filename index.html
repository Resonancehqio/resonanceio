The intelligence layer

Most "AI for housing" is a chatbot
on top of a spreadsheet.
Resonance is something different.

Wrapping a large language model around unstructured data produces confident-sounding answers that can't be trusted with real decisions. Resonance is built on a declarative AI architecture: the system reasons over verified, structured program data to surface actions, flag risks, and coordinate outcomes. Not summaries. Not suggestions. Decisions you can act on.

Declarative AI, not generative guesswork

Generative AI predicts the next likely word. Declarative AI reasons from defined facts, constraints, and rules: and produces outputs that are verifiable, auditable, and traceable to the source data. When Resonance surfaces a match or flags a compliance risk, it shows its work.

Grounded in your program data, not the open internet

Resonance AI operates exclusively over your structured program data: households, units, timelines, eligibility rules, voucher constraints. It doesn't hallucinate. It can't. There's no ambiguity to fill with invention: only structured facts to reason over.

AI that coordinates, not just responds

Most AI tools are reactive: you ask, they answer. Resonance AI is proactive: it monitors your program state, identifies dependency chains, surfaces sequencing risks before they become crises, and recommends the next right action across hundreds of households simultaneously. That's not a chatbot. That's a co-pilot for your program.

The distinction that matters

"The difference between an LLM wrapper and a declarative system isn't speed: it's trust. You can put Resonance's outputs in front of HUD. You can't do that with a summary generated from your inbox."

About Resonance

Operational Real Estate Platform

Resonance is purpose-built affordable housing relocation software designed for housing authorities, affordable housing developers, and relocation service providers. Unlike general property management systems, Resonance is designed specifically for the operational complexity of regulated relocation programs: managing household eligibility, unit readiness, right-to-return commitments, URA compliance documentation, and multi-stakeholder coordination in one connected platform.

RAD & PACT Relocation Management

Resonance supports Rental Assistance Demonstration (RAD) and Permanent Affordability Commitment Together (PACT) relocation programs with purpose-built workflow orchestration. Teams managing RAD conversions and PACT redevelopments use Resonance to coordinate temporary and permanent relocation sequences, track right-to-return eligibility across multi-phase programs, generate HUD-required resident notification documentation, and maintain audit-ready records for program compliance.

URA Compliance & Right-to-Return Tracking

The Uniform Relocation Assistance and Real Property Acquisition Policies Act (URA) imposes strict documentation, notification, and benefit tracking requirements on federally funded relocation programs. Resonance automates URA compliance workflows: tracking relocation benefit eligibility by household, generating required 90-day and 30-day notices, maintaining documented right-to-return rosters, and producing audit-ready compliance records for HUD reviews and funder reporting.

Section 18 & LIHTC Redevelopment Programs

Section 18 disposition programs and Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC) preservation redevelopments involve complex multi-stakeholder coordination across housing authorities, developers, syndicators, and resident services teams. Resonance provides a unified operational platform for managing household case files, unit availability, compliance milestones, capital planning integrations, and program reporting: giving all stakeholders real-time visibility into program status without relying on spreadsheets or email chains.

Housing Authority Operations Software

Housing authorities managing public housing redevelopment programs need operational software purpose-built for their environment: not adapted from commercial property management systems. Resonance gives housing authority program teams a dedicated platform for household case management, relocation workflow coordination, vendor management, compliance documentation, and executive dashboards that provide real-time program status across all active developments and preservation initiatives.

Relocation Service Provider Platform

Relocation service providers (RSPs) coordinating resident moves for housing redevelopment programs manage some of the most operationally complex work in affordable housing. Resonance gives RSPs a single platform to manage household profiles across multiple concurrent programs, coordinate unit placement and matching, track relocation cost disbursements, document URA compliance activities, and report program status to housing authorities, developers, and funders: replacing disconnected spreadsheets and email with a purpose-built operational system.
