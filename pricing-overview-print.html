Resonance Partner Pricing

All contracts are a minimum of one year. Units are contracted at the selected tier level, which covers all programs and projects within it. Multi-year commitments are available at lower rates than those listed below.

Common Questions

Quick answers

How are units counted?
Total units processed across all projects within the contract year. Every unit in every project counts toward the tier — a 300-unit project completed in Q1 and a 200-unit project starting in Q3 both count. Organizations running 15–20 projects annually typically need Tier 2 or Tier 3.
What's the minimum commitment?
One year, at the tier level. Partial-tier billing is not available. Multi-year commitments unlock rates below the listed schedule.
What happens when a project closes?
Units are archived. The full record stays searchable for audit purposes, and a complete documentation package can be exported at any time.
Can small projects share a portal?
Yes. Multiple small projects for the same client or team can share a single project portal, keeping reporting unified and reducing per-project overhead.
What is the implementation timeline?
First project: 3–4 weeks to full configuration. Template-reuse projects: 1-2 week from kickoff to live, depending on project scale. Built around the pace of live programs.
