The problem

Programs are complex.
The tools aren't built for that complexity.

Most teams are running RAD, PACT, and Section 18 programs on spreadsheets, email threads, and disconnected systems. It works — until it doesn't.

Visibility

A funder asks for status. You have no clean answer.

Status buried across five spreadsheet tabs, three email threads, and someone's calendar. A HUD or LIHTC report takes half a day, and is outdated the moment it's done.

Replanning

Construction shifts. The whole plan falls apart.

Phase 2 delays by three weeks. 60 households need re-sequencing. Your team rebuilds the spreadsheet model for a week. Then it changes again Friday.

Compliance

HUD wants Section 18 documentation. You're reconstructing from email.

Every match decision, every right-to-return commitment, every URA benefit: scattered across email and chat. Proving defensibility takes hours. Sometimes it can't be proven at all.

Traditional software tracks what happened.
Resonance coordinates what's next.

What sets us apart

Not a PM tool. Not a CRM.
Purpose-built for this work.

Constraint-aware matching & simulation

Evaluates every unit against bedroom count, accessibility, phase timing, and right-to-return simultaneously. When plans shift, model the response before committing — not after.

Built for team continuity

High turnover doesn't stop programs. New staff see only what's relevant to their role and get up to speed in hours, not weeks. Anyone can pick up where someone else left off — every task, note, and decision is already in the record.

Audit anticipation

Every decision timestamped, attributable, and exportable as it happens. Compliance isn't reconstructed at audit time. It's continuous.
