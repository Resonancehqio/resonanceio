An active unit is any unit currently enrolled in your program — households that have been surveyed, matched, relocated, or are in any phase of active management. Units that have been closed out or archived are not counted. If a project phase ends, those units drop off your count automatically.
Yes. Units can be added at any point. The per-unit rate is calculated on your active count at the end of each billing period. If you add a new program mid-month, we prorate the unit fee for that month.
The implementation fee covers data migration (importing your existing household and unit data), workflow configuration (setting up your specific program structure in Resonance), and team onboarding (training your coordinators to be productive from day one). It's charged once per new program setup and scoped based on the complexity and size of your program. We provide a fixed scope and timeline upfront so there are no surprises.
Yes. Programs that commit to annual contracts receive a discount on both the base fee and the per-unit rate. The volume discount on the per-unit side also increases automatically as your household count grows past the 500 and 1,000 thresholds — so as your portfolio scales, your per-unit cost goes down. Talk to us about your specific situation and we'll structure something that makes sense.

For organizations managing 1,000+ active units across multiple programs, we structure custom pricing around your portfolio's complexity. We work with a range of organizations including nonprofits, housing authorities, for-profit developers, and RSPs. Pricing is based on program size and complexity, not organization type.
