The problem

Your systems tell you what's true. They don't tell you what to do.

Plans break when the schedule moves.

The contractor slips two weeks, and someone spends a week rebuilding the move plan in a spreadsheet.

The reasoning lives in people's heads.

Decisions get made over email and in meetings, and six months later no one can show why.

AI chat tools aren't built for this.

Ask twice and you may get two different answers. You can't take that to a board or to HUD.

FAQ

Questions teams ask first.

Do we have to replace our current systems?

No. DecisionOS reads from the systems and files you already use.

What if the plan is wrong?

A plan is only as good as its rules and data, so DecisionOS makes both visible. If something looks off, your team can see why, fix the rule or the data, and re-plan in minutes. Nothing goes ahead without approval.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

AI models are good at reading messy data, but they can give a different answer each time. DecisionOS uses AI to understand your data and planning engines to make the decision, so the result is repeatable and auditable.

What does it cost?

Pricing is scoped to the decision and the portfolio. Every engagement starts with a short pilot on your own data.
