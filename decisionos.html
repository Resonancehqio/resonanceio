FAQ

Questions teams ask first.

Do we have to replace our current systems?

No. DecisionOS reads from the systems and files you already use.

Do we need Resonance to use DecisionOS?

No. DecisionOS works on its own, on top of the systems you have now. If you use Resonance, DecisionOS reads from it like any other source.

What if the plan is wrong?

A plan is only as good as its rules and data, so DecisionOS makes both visible. If something looks off, your team can see why. Fix the rule or the data, and re-plan in minutes. Nothing goes ahead without approval.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

AI models are good at reading messy data, but they can give a different answer each time. DecisionOS uses AI to understand your data and planning engines to make the decision. The result is repeatable and auditable.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the decision and the portfolio. Every engagement starts with a short pilot on your own data.
