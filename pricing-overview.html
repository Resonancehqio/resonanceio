Common Questions

Quick answers

What counts as an "active unit"?
Any unit currently enrolled in your program — surveyed, matched, in relocation, or being managed. Closed-out or archived units are not counted.
Can units be added mid-month?
Yes. The per-unit fee is calculated on your active count at end of billing period. New programs added mid-month are prorated.
What if our program ends?
Units are archived and removed from your active count. Between programs, we can discuss reduced-rate archive tiers or account hold options.
Do you offer nonprofit / PHA pricing?
Pricing is based on program size, not organization type. We're happy to structure payment timing around HUD funding cycles.
