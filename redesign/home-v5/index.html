What it does

One platform. Multiple decision apps.

Resonance reads the data you already have, maps it to your business structure, and helps teams decide what to do next.

It is built for organizations where one delay can affect cost, delivery, compliance, and customer commitments.

How it works

From raw data to action in three steps

  1. 01Connect your data

    Bring in ERP exports, Excel sheets, and operational systems without rebuilding your stack.

  2. 02Map the business reality

    Resonance builds an ontology of your operation so the system understands your roles, objects, constraints, and workflows.

  3. 03Generate decisions

    The platform surfaces findings, recommends actions, and keeps evidence attached to every decision.

Product philosophy

The AI is not the product. The decision system is.

Large language models are useful for translation and interaction. But the real value comes from the underlying ontology, rules, constraints, and decision logic that make the output usable in production.

Resonance is designed for hard problems where a good demo is not enough.
