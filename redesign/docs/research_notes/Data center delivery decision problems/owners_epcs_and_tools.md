## 5. Vocabulary
Note: these one-line meanings are standard construction-management usage written from general knowledge, not from fetched sources, except where a term appeared in a fetched page (baseline, change order, delay analysis, long-lead procurement, master budget).
- EAC (estimate at completion): forecast total final cost = actual cost to date + ETC.
- ETC (estimate to complete): forecast cost still to be spent.
- Baseline / re-baseline: the approved schedule or budget used for measuring variance / formally resetting it after approved changes or major disruption.
- Contingency drawdown: releasing held risk money into the budget to cover realized risks or scope gaps.
- Allowance: placeholder budget for scope not yet defined, trued up at buy-out.
- Change order: signed modification to contract price, time or scope.
- RFI: written question to design team seeking clarification of documents.
- Submittal: contractor-provided product/shop-drawing data for design approval.
- OAC meeting: owner-architect-contractor coordination meeting, typically weekly.
- Pull planning: backward-from-milestone collaborative scheduling by trades.
- Look-ahead schedule: rolling 3-6 week detailed field schedule.
- Long-lead item: equipment (switchgear, transformers, generators, chillers) with procurement time on the critical path.
- Owner's representative: owner-side advisor managing the GC, budget, schedule and approvals (see CBRE posting).
- Delay analysis: assessing time impact of changes or events on the critical path (term in CBRE posting).
