## How it works

H2: **From your exports to a signed decision**

**Data agents.** The agents read what you already export from SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, P6 and Excel, without reformatting. They match values across files, flag gaps and conflicts, and check every mapping before anything uses it.

**Your industry's model.** A data center job is packages, crews, gates and handover dates. A distribution network is orders, lanes and service targets. We start from the model for your industry and add your rules on top, so no customer starts from zero.

**Solvers.** Deterministic math works out which options are possible, what each costs and how many days it buys. If an option breaks a rule, the solver rules it out and shows why.

**Decision apps.** Each decision goes to the person who owns it, with the options and the evidence behind each. They approve one, and the record keeps who approved it and why.

## Why not a chatbot
