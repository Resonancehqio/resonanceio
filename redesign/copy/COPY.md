## 3. The problem

H2: **The gear is late. Now what?**

- If the switchgear is late, the handover is late, and the first question is whose day it is.
- Every day costs standby, extended general conditions or damages, and nobody priced it the week the gear slipped.
- When the claim comes, the record is spread across six inboxes and a spreadsheet one person can read.

## 4. How it works

H2: **Three steps, on top of the systems you already run.**

1. **A package slips.** Resonance shows what that does to the handover date and the money.
2. **You see the options you can build.** Each one has a cost, the days it saves, and the source for every figure.
3. **One named person approves.** The record keeps who approved it, and why.

Line under: P6, SAP, Oracle, Procore and Excel stay. Resonance reads what they already hold.

## 6. Questions you'll have

**"My schedulers already do this."**
They still own the schedule. Resonance does the repeat work when a package slips, and every figure carries a record ID they can check.

**"We're not replacing P6."**
Nothing is replaced. Resonance sits on top of P6, SAP, Oracle, Procore and Excel. Your records stay where they are.

**"We already use ChatGPT."**
Keep using it. Here a language model only drafts text. The math runs on solvers, and a named person approves every decision.
